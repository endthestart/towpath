# Week plan: October 12–16, 2026

Status: agreed Monday, October 12. Measurements and deployment details are kept in the owner's private
operational record; this plan stays generic.

## Purpose

The first full folder index and the mail indexes are running on the owner's server. This week puts something
usable behind three goals: find anything in files and mail; know what is useless in storage; and take a first
step toward the life story. Mail cleanup belongs to the mail-management tool ([D15](../decisions.md),
[mail management](../mail-management.md)) and is not part of this week. The week also sets a predictable rhythm.

## Rules

- **At most one release a day**, deployed only while indexing is idle. Each day has a required outcome and
  optional work; unfinished work moves forward by priority and optional work may drop out of the week.
- **Done means qualified on the server.** Timing targets are measurements to qualify, not promises made by a
  query's shape. Every new query is timed for a common result, a rare result, an empty result and a later page.
- **No expensive work at service startup.** Filling search indexes, backfills and index builds on large tables
  run as resumable worker jobs with progress and Pause.
- **Catalog rules.** Pages read stored figures; no query sorts the whole catalog; `AFTER UPDATE OF` triggers are
  guarded with `WHEN old.x IS NOT new.x`.
- **Rollback is tested, not assumed.** On days that change a schema, the previous image runs against copies of
  stores the new image upgraded: writes succeed, owner decisions survive, pages load.
- **Claims stay as narrow as the evidence.** Same name and size is a duplicate candidate; "no indexed members"
  does not mean an archive was never opened; file-modified and mail-header dates describe source activity, not
  life events; unknown stays unknown.
- **Figures and searches agree exactly** under the same snapshot and counting rule, defined once.
- No setting changes without review. CPU-bound hot paths are noted for Rust ([roadmap](../roadmap.md)).
